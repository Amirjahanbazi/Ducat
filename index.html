Oracle

Price locks on Bitcoin

Ducat's Chainlink CRE oracle uses HMAC to derive a secret for each price threshold. Only a hash of it is committed on Bitcoin. Move the price below the line to see the sequence.

Conceptual illustration, not a live price feed.

1
Secret heldOnly its hash is recorded on Bitcoin. The secret stays private while the price is at or above the threshold.
2
Threshold secret revealedThe price has crossed below the line, so the oracle reveals that threshold's secret.
3
Bitcoin verifies, guardians co-signBitcoin checks the secret against the committed hash. Guardian signatures are still required to release collateral.

FAQ

Questions about borrowing

What is UNIT?

UNIT is Ducat's bitcoin-backed stablecoin, designed to track the US dollar. Borrowers lock BTC collateral to mint it. How UNIT works

How much can I borrow?

It depends on your BTC collateral and the vault's collateral ratio. Review the available amount, fees and liquidation price in the app before confirming. The calculator above gives an estimate from the documented parameters.

When can I get my bitcoin back?

Repay the outstanding debt, then redeem the collateral through a Bitcoin transaction. If your vault reaches its liquidation threshold first, its collateral can be liquidated.

Is the BTC Strategy Vault available?

Not yet. It is in development. It proposes sharing minting fees, managed liquidation income and managed liquidity-pool fees with depositors, with payouts in BTC. Income would be variable and depositors would share losses.
